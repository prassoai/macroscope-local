## Design Context

### Users
Developers installing or updating the Macroscope CLI while working in a terminal with Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, or OpenCode. They need to understand what will change, keep control of integrations, and finish setup quickly.

### Brand Personality
Precise, calm, and technically confident. The installer should feel like the Murmur CLI: compact, legible, trustworthy, and quietly polished.

### Aesthetic Direction
A restrained terminal-native interface with strong hierarchy and minimal preamble. Cyan is the primary navigational accent, green communicates selected and successful states, yellow is reserved for caution or inspectable alternatives, and dim terminal text carries secondary guidance. Progress appears only when it reflects real underlying work.

### Design Principles
- Show only sections that require a user decision.
- Make selected, active, successful, warning, and error states distinguishable without relying on color alone.
- Prefer truthful native progress output over synthetic percentages.
- Keep commands, plans, and filesystem effects easy to scan.
- Respect `NO_COLOR` and terminal-default foreground/background colors.
